Hardcover Monograph • Typography

Simplicity is not a style.
It is an intentional restraint.

Derived from the Japanese aesthetic principle of Kanso (簡素), my practice eliminates non-essential elements to express the underlying truth of a brand, architectural structure, or text.

I work closely with architects, luxury ateliers, and independent technologists who value enduring clarity over temporary digital trends. Each project is grounded in strict typographic hierarchy, natural tactile materials, and lightning-fast static code.
